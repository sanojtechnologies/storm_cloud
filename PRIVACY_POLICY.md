# Privacy Policy for Storm Cloud

**Last Updated**: November 2, 2026
**Published by**: Sanoj Technologies

## Summary

Storm Cloud is an action game in which you play a storm cloud. Your progress is saved on your phone only. There are no accounts, and no servers of ours receive your data.

The game is free to download. A one-time in-app purchase through Google Play unlocks the full game. It can show an optional ad from Google AdMob, which you choose to watch for a second chance during a run. Google's ad software collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

## Your game data stays on your device

Storm Cloud saves the following on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

- **Progress**: the arenas and intensity levels you have unlocked, your best times, your rain balance, permanent upgrades, unlocked clouds, achievements and the evolutions you have discovered
- **Daily storm**: your best score for the day and your streak
- **Totals**: how many runs you have played, invaders defeated and wins
- **Settings**: sound, screen shake and bright flashes on or off
- **Play time**: how many days and sessions you have played, and for how long

None of this is uploaded or sent to us.

- **Uninstalling:** removes everything the game stored.
- **Phone backup:** if you use your phone's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account), your phone may restore this progress on a new or reset device. That backup is handled by your phone and Google, not by us.
- **Rain:** has no real-world value and cannot be bought.

## Purchase (Google Play Billing)

The full-game unlock is sold and processed by Google Play.

- We never see your payment details.
- The game asks Google Play whether your account owns the unlock, so it can turn it on. It also does this when you tap **Restore purchase**.
- Google processes purchase data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads only appear if you choose them: when your cloud is defeated, you can watch an optional ad for a second chance. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following automatically:

- **Device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and app set ID
- **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general location
- **App interactions**: for example app launches, taps and ad views
- **Diagnostic information**: for example app launch time and performance

On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving ad services (such as Topics and attribution reporting).

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads and purchases need an internet connection. Playing the game never does.

**Consent**: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with **Ad privacy options** in Settings.

**Your choices**: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

## Sharing

When you tap **Share** after a daily storm, the game opens your phone's share menu with a short text: your score, the arena and your streak. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: for ads and purchases only
- **Billing**: for the full-game purchase through Google Play
- **Advertising ID and ad services**: used by Google's ads SDK, as described above

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is under your control, as described above. For data processed by Google for advertising and purchases, see Google's privacy policy and controls.

For users in the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above.
