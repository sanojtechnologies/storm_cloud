# Privacy Policy for Storm Cloud

**Last Updated**: November 2, 2026
**Published by**: Sanoj Technologies

## Summary

Storm Cloud is an action game in which you play a storm cloud. Your progress is saved on your phone only. There are no accounts, and no servers of ours receive your data.

The game is free, with every arena and intensity level included. Its only income is optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. Google's ad software collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

## Your game data stays on your device

Storm Cloud saves the following on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

- **Progress**: the arenas and intensity levels you have unlocked, your best times, your rain balance, permanent upgrades, cloud colours, unlocked clouds, achievements and the evolutions you have discovered
- **Daily storm**: your best score for the day and your streak
- **Totals**: how many runs you have played, invaders defeated and wins
- **Settings**: sound, screen shake, bright flashes and left-handed buttons on or off
- **Play time**: how many days and sessions you have played, and for how long

None of this is uploaded or sent to us.

- **Reset progress (in the game's Settings):** erases your progress on this phone. Your settings and play time stay.
- **Uninstalling:** removes everything the game stored.
- **Phone backup:** if you use your phone's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account), your phone may restore this progress on a new or reset device. That backup is handled by your phone and Google, not by us.
- **Rain:** has no real-world value and cannot be bought. Watching an optional ad can earn it, like playing does.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads only appear if you choose them. Every ad is a rewarded ad: a button says what it pays, you decide whether to watch, and nothing in the game is held back if you don't. The four places are:

- **Second chance**: when your cloud is defeated, watch an ad to continue the run (once per run, not in the daily storm)
- **Double your rain**: on the results screen, watch an ad to double the rain that run earned (once per run)
- **Daily rain barrel**: in the menu, watch an ad once a day for a rain gift
- **Boosted start**: in the menu, watch an ad to begin your next run with an extra free upgrade pick

If an ad cannot be shown, the button says so and no benefit is granted, except the second chance, which the game grants anyway. Closing an ad before it finishes earns nothing.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following automatically:

- **Device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and app set ID
- **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general location
- **App interactions**: for example app launches, taps and ad views
- **Diagnostic information**: for example app launch time and performance

On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving ad services (such as Topics and attribution reporting).

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads need an internet connection. Playing the game never does.

**Consent**: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with **Ad privacy options** in Settings.

**Your choices**: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

## Sharing

When you tap **Share** after a daily storm, the game opens your phone's share menu with a short text: your score, the arena, your streak and a link to the game. If no share menu is available, the text is copied to your clipboard instead. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: for ads only
- **Advertising ID and ad services**: used by Google's ads SDK, as described above

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is under your control, as described above. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and controls.

For users in the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above.
